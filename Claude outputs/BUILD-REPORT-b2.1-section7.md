## 4. Known gaps and the authoring TODO

1. **Reading sits at ~2% against a 5% target in both sections.** This is honest
   rather than fixable by generating more items: at A1 there is very little text
   long enough to read. The fix is graded readers in the Immersion Engine
   (docs/06 §7), not more exercises.
2. **Grammar items are ~4 points below target.** Each grammar point now has
   exactly one home node, which is correct; the shortfall should be closed with
   hand-authored contrast items rather than more generated variants.
3. **Item volume runs at roughly 60-70% of the modelled lesson time.** Each
   node-tier averages ~13 items where ~16-18 are needed. The remainder should be
   hand-authored variety, not more of the same templates.
4. **All audio is referenced but not recorded.** Human voices are required below
   C1 (docs/15 §5).
5. **No images yet.** `mcq_image` and `listen_choose_image` are unused for that
   reason; at A1 they matter, and image sourcing is the next media task.
6. **B2.1 is authored.** Section 7 added 36 grammar points (G-207…G-242),
   9 phonology points (P-061…P-069), 18 functions (F-095…F-112), 912 lexemes,
   18 story episodes and 54 comprehension questions. Its syllabus, like B1's,
   had to be written from scratch.
7. **The B1.2 lexeme shortfall did not repeat at B2.** Section 6 could only
   find 626 genuinely new lexemes against a nominal 800; Section 7 reached 912
   at 50 per unit without padding, because academic, legal, journalistic and
   argumentative registers open at B2 (docs/00 §2.2) and bring a wide vocabulary
   with them. The lesson stands: derive the per-section target from what is left
   in the lexicon, do not copy a flat number down the levels.
8. **Section 7 is the first section to satisfy docs/00 §4.1 rule 7.** Every B2.1
   lexeme carries an English definition as well as an L1 gloss. No lexeme in the
   course had one before. New gate G25 blocks a B2-or-later record without one
   and reports the 1,425-record B1 backlog rather than pretending it complies.
9. **Another 60 everyday words were missing from the course entirely** — the same
   class Section 6 exposed, and exactly what gate G24 had been reporting from the
   corpus. Three findings stand out. **Every ordinal above 'third' was absent**,
   so a learner could not say "the fourth of June". **Ten verbs were missing whose
   noun the course already taught** — `own`, `reply`, `promise`, `lead`, `mark`,
   `cause`, `head`, `review`, `name`, `permit` — a systematic pattern, not ten
   accidents. And a further layer of plain nouns, adjectives and adverbs was never
   taught at all: `parent`, `limit`, `matter`, `top`, `bottom`, `building`, `act`,
   `count`, `post`, `cover`, `edge`, `pressure`, `over`, `within`, `perfect`,
   `recent`, `essential`, `accurate`, `successful`, `rarely`, `directly`, `merely`.
   All are now taught where a learner first needs them
   (`content/lexemes/src-gap-topup-2.json`).
10. **Three builder defects were generating English that does not exist.**
   `agree` produced `agreeed` (a verb ending in -ee takes a bare -d); `parents`
   and `customs` were taught as plural-only nouns and re-pluralised to
   `parentses` and `customses`; and `odd_one_out` could offer the same word twice
   when a lemma is taught as both noun and verb — which the gap top-up made
   common by adding `count`, `post`, `act`, `matter`, `limit` and `cover` in both
   parts of speech. All three are fixed at source, and `parents`/`matters` were
   replaced by the singular entries whose inflections already produce them.
11. **The frequency band is now snapped onto the schema ladder by the builder.**
   docs/04 §6 skips 7000, so an authored estimate of "about 7000" produced
   records the schema rejected — twice in two sections. `build_lexemes.py` now
   snaps to the next band up and prints what it changed, instead of emitting an
   invalid record.
12. **193 band-1000 lexemes are still first taught at B1 or later** — gate G24,
   unchanged from Section 6. Section 7's gap top-up cleared the words its own
   stories needed, but the backlog itself is a Sections 1-4 job.
13. **Section 8 (B2.2 'Abstraction & Register', units 103-120) is next** — 18
   units, and the last section before the C-levels. The pipeline needs no
   changes; the B2.2 syllabus does have to be authored from scratch.

## 5. How to rebuild

```
./build.sh
```

which runs, in order:

```
python3 tools/build_lexemes.py   # compact sources  -> build/lexemes.json
python3 tools/build_units.py     # unit specs       -> build/units/*.json
python3 tools/build_items.py     # everything above -> build/items/*.json
python3 tools/validate.py        # the CI gates (exit 1 blocks a merge)
python3 tools/report.py          # this file
```

Builds are deterministic (`random.seed(20260908)`), so the same sources always
produce the same item ids.
